QUANTUM CIRCUIT WORKSHOP

Quantum Foundry

Build sixteen missions, turn your solutions into reusable blocks, and grow from a wire to registers and quantum algorithms.

Launch game →
  1. Choose First Connection on the map.
  2. Read the briefing, then click Build circuit.
  3. Connect INPUT A’s right pin to OUTPUT A’s left pin. Click Check circuit.

A briefing opens before every mission and replay. Reopen Assignment for its circuit diagram and guided experiment. For an immediate working example, choose Bell demo. Click ♫ for original music; the menu has volume and track controls. Sandbox lets you choose one to six qubits.

Unzip the package and double-click index.html in Safari or Chrome. The playable file is self-contained: no installation, server, account, or internet connection is needed.

Menu ≡ → Download backup saves portable progress. Import backup restores it, including backups from the earlier version-3 prototype. Download a backup before replacing an older copy if you want to preserve its progress.

The optional Mac launcher begins with #!/bin/bash. If macOS refuses it, open index.html directly.
