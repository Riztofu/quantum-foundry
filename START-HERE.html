QUANTUM CIRCUIT WORKSHOP

Quantum Foundry

Build sixteen missions, save your circuits as reusable blocks, and grow from a wire to quantum algorithms.

Launch game →
  1. Choose First Connection on the map.
  2. Read the briefing. It explains Hadamard and CNOT before you play.
  3. Connect INPUT A’s right pin to OUTPUT A’s left pin, then choose Check circuit.

A briefing opens before every mission and replay. Reopen Assignment for a worked example and a short experiment. Choose Bell demo for an example circuit. Click ♫ for music; the menu has ten Kevin MacLeod tracks and volume controls. Sandbox lets you choose one to six qubits and has no Check circuit button.

Unzip the package and open index.html in Safari or Chrome. Keep the assets/music folder beside it for offline playback of all music. The game needs no account or internet connection.

Menu ≡ → Download backup saves portable progress. Import backup restores it, including backups from the earlier version-3 prototype. Download a backup before replacing an older copy if you want to preserve its progress.

The optional Mac launcher begins with #!/bin/bash. If macOS refuses it, open index.html directly.
